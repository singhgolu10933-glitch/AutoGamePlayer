Code of Conduct

Our Commitment

We want AutoGamePlayer to be a respectful and constructive open-source project.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to communicate respectfully and contribute in good faith.

Expected Behavior

Contributors should:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Provide useful and technically relevant feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience and perspectives.

Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Sharing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Malicious or abusive behavior toward project participants.

Project Scope

Contributions must remain within the legitimate purpose of AutoGamePlayer.

Do not submit changes intended to bypass anti-cheat systems, DRM, authentication controls, access controls, or other protections of third-party games.

Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the repository's available GitHub communication channels.

Reports will be handled as reasonably and appropriately as possible.

Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove, reject, or restrict contributions or participation that violate this Code of Conduct.

The goal is to maintain a safe, respectful, and technically productive project community.
